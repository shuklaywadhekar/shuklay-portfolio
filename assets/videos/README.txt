# Assets Guide — Videos

Place your local video files in this directory.

### Key Video File:
- `hero.mp4` — Muted cinematic background video for the full-screen Hero section.

### Recommendation for Web Video:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio or muted)
- Resolution: 1080p (1920x1080) or 720p
- Bitrate: 2-4 Mbps for fast loading over mobile connections
- Duration: 10-30 second seamless loop
