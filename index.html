Biography & Vision

BEHIND THE CAMERA

Shuklay Wadhekar is a Mumbai-based photographer, cinematographer and documentary filmmaker working across films, advertising, entertainment, brands and real-world storytelling.

His approach combines keen human observation, disciplined cinematic composition, and an obsession with authentic light to create imagery that feels timeless, emotional, and visceral. Whether operating in high-pressure stadium tours, intimate film sets, or remote cultural communities, his focus remains steadfast on truthful storytelling.
